I started by asking questions of data. Now I build the systems that make those answers possible.

My career has moved through research, data analysis, business intelligence and BI leadership into cloud data engineering. That journey shaped how I work: I care about the infrastructure beneath the dashboard just as much as the decision made from it.

I enjoy the part of engineering where technology meets judgment — designing systems that are reproducible, observable and resilient enough to evolve with the people who depend on them.

HOW I THINK

Good engineering is not just about what works today.

01

Reliability before sophistication.

A system people trust beats a clever system nobody can operate.

02

Design for change.

Schemas evolve. Teams scale. Requirements shift. Architecture should expect that.

03

Infrastructure should be reproducible.

If it cannot be recreated predictably, it eventually becomes operational debt.
