About Systems Thinking Lab

ABOUT / CAREER PATH / HOW I WORK

I’ve spent enough time near decisions, dashboards, and delivery to care deeply about what holds them up.

My background moved from research into analytics, then business intelligence, and eventually into cloud data engineering. That progression shaped the way I see platform work: technical quality matters most when it meaningfully supports other people’s work.

THE THREAD

From asking questions of data to building the systems that help answer them.

I started in research, where rigor and validation mattered. Then I moved into analytical roles, where the value of data was tied to clarity, timeliness, and trust. Leading BI work made another layer visible: it is not enough to produce numbers. The path those numbers travel matters too.

That is part of what drew me toward engineering. I became increasingly interested in the infrastructure under the dashboard, the orchestration under the report, and the design decisions that make data work resilient instead of brittle.

WHAT I CARE ABOUT

  • Cloud-native data platforms that remain understandable over time
  • Reliable orchestration, clean transformations, and reproducible deployment
  • Lakehouse reliability, observability, and schema-change impact
  • Building systems that support good decisions without adding unnecessary noise
