ABOUT / CAREER / APPROACH

From analysing data
to engineering the platform.

I am a Cloud Data Engineer with a background that spans research, analytics, BI leadership and cloud platform delivery.

That progression matters because it changed how I see data engineering. I have worked close to the people consuming the output, led analysts responsible for reporting quality, and now build the infrastructure and pipelines underneath those experiences.

Today my focus is cloud-native data platforms: AWS infrastructure, Snowflake, dbt, Airflow, containerised workloads, Infrastructure as Code and the reliability problems that appear as systems scale.

I am especially interested in data platform engineering, lakehouse reliability, observability and the infrastructure required to make analytics and AI dependable.
