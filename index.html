I didn’t start out in engineering.

I started in research, moved into analytics and BI, and eventually became more interested in the infrastructure beneath the dashboards than the dashboards themselves.

That path still shapes how I work. I care about technical quality, but I also care about whether the people using a system can understand it, depend on it and change it without unnecessary friction.
